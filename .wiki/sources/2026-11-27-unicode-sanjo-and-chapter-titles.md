# Carry the print into the Xerox record

The creator found the transitions awkward, requested a Korean sanjo-style score for the Unicode film and flagged “글꼴에서 시작된 일” as an unnatural title. This revises the previous release. [[2026-11-27-unicode-chronology-release]]

The enlarged print now moves into the first exhibit. The date and paper header retain their positions. The incoming labels appear as the exhibit clears their space. Consecutive moving exhibits use the recorded motion directly. Xerox and Apple have separate titles that name their work. The wording check rejects “글꼴에서 시작된 일” and permits “서울에서 시작된 일정”. [Camera](../../episodes/unicode-1.0/film_opening.py) · [Chapter titles](../../episodes/unicode-1.0/produce.py) · [Copy tests](../../tests/korean-copy.test.ts)

The original score uses a synthesized plucked string with pitch inflections and two synthesized drum heads. A free opening leads into a measured pulse when the film reaches Xerox. Each edition has its own closing phrase. The score draws on the National Gugak Center’s account of gayageum sanjo.
