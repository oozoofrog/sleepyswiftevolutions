# Introducing the first drawing

The creator asked for transitions at each change and asked how to introduce the first drawing. [Creator request](t3-thread://v2/4976b4a8-e1c4-4543-877c-25d62dfa39d1/2753ea1c-ac4d-4db4-bcc0-2e5b727de699)

The SE-0005 production choice is to fade chapter content through the stable background, then reveal the paper, point and pencil in that order. The initial move changes the current point. A later drawing cue adds the path. [Landscape excerpt](../../episodes/SE-0005/out/SE-0005-drawing-entrance-ko.mp4)
