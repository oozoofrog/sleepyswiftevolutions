# Unicode glyphs inside the stroke

The creator asked to make the enlarged stroke consist of Unicode characters. This changes the subject of the final magnification in [[2026-11-27-unicode-continuous-zoom]]. The camera now reveals three glyph masks clipped to the scanned ink contour. The glyphs and their code points share fixed page coordinates. The maximum magnification holds long enough to read them before the camera pulls back.

The editor selected 一 (U+4E00), 靑 (U+9751) and 青 (U+9752) from the two charts already featured in the film. The small glyphs use those actual printed forms. Their arrangement and the Menlo labels are an editorial illustration. The Korean narration and captions now describe the characters and their reunion into one stroke. [Mapping table, printed page 171](https://www.unicode.org/versions/Unicode1.0.0/HanCharts2.pdf#page=3) · [Character chart, printed page 98](https://www.unicode.org/versions/Unicode1.0.0/HanCharts1.pdf#page=76)

The updated shot runs for 335 frames at 24 fps. The inward and outward camera paths match at five sample magnifications, and the first and last image regions match.
