# Continuous zoom into the printed stroke

The creator asked for the magnification to reveal finer detail continuously like an infinite zoom painting. In the Unicode film this supersedes the pixel-step enlargement in [[2026-11-27-unicode-scan-grain]]. The recorded scan still defines the printed stroke and visible gray marks. [Mapping table in volume 2, printed page 171](https://www.unicode.org/versions/Unicode1.0.0/HanCharts2.pdf#page=3) · [Previous pixel enlargement](../../episodes/unicode-1.0/motion/grain-native.mp4)

The camera moves from the printed row into the upper contour of the stroke. Four registered plates reveal finer irregularities and paper fibres as the visible area narrows. The shot reaches 128 times its opening magnification and retraces the lens movement. The on-screen label identifies subpixel details as an illustration. In the reader, use 원본 대조 to compare the recorded scan at the same framing.
