# Keep visible content in motion across layouts

The creator asked to follow existing code, charts and other content to their next position when another item joins them. The new item should emerge from a concealed position. [Creator feedback](t3-thread://v2/4976b4a8-e1c4-4543-877c-25d62dfa39d1/2753ea1c-ac4d-4db4-bcc0-2e5b727de699)

The editor chose a 1.25-second move for SE-0005. The code travels from its actual outgoing bounds. A mask on the edge facing the code reveals the paper or comparison panel. The landscape edition places the paper to the right. The portrait edition places it above the code. The pencil and the mark keep their narration cues.
