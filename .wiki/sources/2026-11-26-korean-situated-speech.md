# Korean expressions in their social settings

The creator found the scripts implausible and the metaphors unclear. This first pass examines a greeting, an idiom, a routine request and a specialist practice. The sources establish particular uses. They do not measure everyday usage frequency or preferences by age or region.

| Expression | Evidence and setting | Editorial inference for SE-0003 |
|---|---|---|
| 밥 먹었어요? | A National Institute of Korean Language teacher-materials development report treats this expression as a greeting and treats arranging lunch as a separate activity. | The time of day, relationship and next turn establish whether the speaker asks after someone or intends to feed them. Neither use entails copying a value. [Teaching materials, printed p. 27](https://www.korean.go.kr/common/download.do?c_file_name=cef92c70-c355-439c-b6d7-fd5ca359b630.pdf&file_path=reportData&o_file_name=2014.pdf) |
| 손이 크다 | The dictionary includes generosity with resources and an example of serving guests plenty of food. | The utterance characterizes a person. Doubling a numeral loses its social point. [Korean Basic Dictionary, 손](https://krdict.korean.go.kr/eng/dicSearch/SearchView?ParaWordNo=17608&nation=eng) |
| 원본을 복사하다 / 한 부씩 | In a Daejeon Jung-gu council meeting, a member who had received a contract asked if the copy had been reduced, then requested copies of the original. A separate Gyeongnam council record documents a request for one set per member. | The request has a recipient and an immediate reason. The minutes document distribution. A recipient annotating their own copy would be a fictional extension. [Jung-gu minutes](https://council.djjunggu.go.kr/viewer/minutes/6310.do) · [Gyeongnam minutes](https://council.gyeongnam.go.kr/record/recordView.do?key=dd5758f8543b731e620a1644f9cdba9a8d35b9faa5ba26d1766909d6ac4c31de326c452663378014) |
| 복기하다 / 이렇게 두었으면 | Professional player Lee Da-hye explains reviewing a finished game to see where a choice went wrong. Korea Baduk Association reporting records players discussing a move they missed. | Recreate a position, then try a different move out of regret or curiosity. The game record still contains the played move. The stone count, score and historical record do not correspond to the integer literal. [Lee Da-hye interview](https://m.radio.ytn.co.kr/interview_view.php?id=41698&s_mcd=0263) · [Korea Baduk Association match report](https://m.baduk.or.kr/news/B02_view.asp?news_sum_no=322) |
