# Unicode chronology release and Korean wording

The creator asked to begin with a modern character, follow its stroke into a pile of weathered type and introduce the 1980s records during the zoom. The editor chose 一 and used print texture from the first edition to reconstruct the close view. The archival label identifies the reconstruction. The next scene introduces the Xerox font work. [Official chronology](https://www.unicode.org/history/versionone.html) · [Optical reference](2026-11-27-unicode-optical-print.md)

The creator also asked for a lint rule that rejects phrases such as “뭐가 들어가느자리”. The revised line reads “대만 문자표에서는 두 글자의 번호가 달랐습니다.” The closing chapter names the operation “원래 문자표로 되돌리기”. The rule checks chapter titles, narration and captions, including the Short. It permits literal terms such as “가장자리” and “소수점 셋째 자리”.
