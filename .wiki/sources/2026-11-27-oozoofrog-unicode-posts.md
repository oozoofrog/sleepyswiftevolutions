# oozoofrog’s posts on early Unicode

In the 20 March 2019 post, oozoofrog follows the Unicode Consortium chronology from 1986 through 1992 and adds personal notes. The July 1991 entry connects the decision to split the book with coordination on ISO/IEC 10646. The October entry records the arrival of Volume 1 and the June 1992 entry records the printing of Volume 2. [유니코드 1.0 연대기](https://oozoofrog.dev/blog/chronology_unicode/) · [Consortium chronology](https://www.unicode.org/history/versionone.html)

The 13 April 2018 post introduces the early design goals and the investigations behind the fixed-width proposal. The author’s October 2026 note distinguishes the initial design from modern UTF-8 and UTF-16 and clarifies round-trip conversion. [유니코드(Unicode)](https://oozoofrog.dev/blog/unicode/)
